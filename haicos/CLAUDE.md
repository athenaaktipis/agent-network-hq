# HAICoS workspace — context

HAICoS is the **Human-AI Coevolution Society**, a new scholarly society Athena Aktipis is
founding (Founding President). It brings together researchers and practitioners across
cooperation science, evolutionary biology/psychology, complexity science, and the AI
industry around the study of how humans and AI systems shape each other's evolution.
It grew out of conversations at the ISEEC meeting in Crete (late May 2026); the society
and its website (haicos.org, on Hostinger) were set up starting June 10, 2026.

This workspace is for society operations. It lives inside the (public) agent-network-hq
repo but is a separate project from the Schmidt grant page — do not mix the two. The
repo-root CLAUDE.md's rules about `index.html` and `/sync` do not govern this directory.
